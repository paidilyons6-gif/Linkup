What you get

A page at an address of your choosing, editable by you, on whichever plan you're paying for. Building a page is free; publishing one needs a paid plan. What each plan includes is on the pricing page and may change with a month's notice.

Paying

Plans are monthly, in euro, charged in advance, and renew automatically until you cancel. Prices include VAT where it applies. You can cancel at any time from Manage billing — you keep access until the end of the month you've paid for, and part-months aren't refunded.

If a payment fails we'll retry. If it keeps failing your page stops being public, but nothing is deleted — pay and it comes straight back.

Your first fourteen days: as a consumer buying online in the EU you have fourteen days to change your mind. If you publish a page inside that window you're asking us to start straight away, and the refund is reduced by what you've used. Ask and we'll sort it out fairly.

What you put on your page

Your content stays yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to people who visit your page — that's all, and it ends when you delete it.

You're responsible for what you publish. Don't put up anything illegal, anything that isn't yours to use, anything designed to deceive, or anything that would make us a party to it. We can take down a page that breaks this, and will tell you why.

Email

On the Business plan you can email the people who signed up through your page. You may only email people who actually gave you their address there. Uploading a list you got somewhere else is not allowed and will get your account closed — it damages delivery for every other customer.

There is a monthly ceiling on how many emails an account can send. It's generous for normal use; if you need more, ask.

Ending it

You can delete your account whenever you like, and it takes everything with it. We can close an account that breaks these terms, or give thirty days' notice for any other reason and refund what you've paid for time you won't get.

What we don't promise

We work to keep the service running but can't promise it's never down. We're not liable for money you didn't make, and in any case our liability is limited to what you've paid us in the previous twelve months. Nothing here limits liability that can't legally be limited.

Changes

If these terms change materially we'll email you at least thirty days beforehand. Carrying on using LinkUp after that means accepting the new version; if you'd rather not, cancel and we'll refund the unused part.

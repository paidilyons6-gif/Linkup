Questions

Straight answers.

Is this only for fitness coaches?

No. Any creator who sells programmes, courses, or offers can build a quiz. Fitness is a natural fit — pregnancy, goals, experience level — but the builder is open-ended.

Do you take a cut of my sales?

No. LinkUp never processes your programme checkout. Visitors leave to your Stripe, Shopify, Kajabi, or whatever you already use. You keep 100%.

Can I still use a normal link list?

Yes. Switch to Hub mode in Studio, or add destinations to your quiz. You can add several Instagram links, one per profile or post.

Is building free?

Yes. Build and preview everything. You only pay when you publish on your address.

Do website builds charge online?

No. Sales pages and full sites are quoted and invoiced by email — see how it works. In-app payments are only for LinkUp plans (Launch / Guide / Scale) when you publish.

Do Scale broadcasts work out of the box?

Email capture on your page works with Scale. Sending broadcasts needs a verified sending domain (Resend) configured by us — until then the send button explains that email isn’t configured yet.

Questions

Do I need a website?

No. LinkUp is the website. You put one link in your Instagram or TikTok bio and change what's behind it whenever you want.

What if I hate being on camera?

Leave the welcome video out and the page opens straight onto your bubbles. You can add one later — it takes fifteen seconds and it's the thing people remember.

Can I change it after it's live?

As often as you like. Edit it on your phone and it's updated for everyone immediately. The link never changes.

What happens to my email list if I leave?

You export it and take it with you. Addresses people gave you are yours, not ours.

Is there a free trial?

Building a page is free — you only pay when you want it live on your own address. Your subscription starts when you complete checkout.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this only for fitness coaches?

No. Any creator who sells programmes, courses, or offers can build a quiz. Fitness is a natural fit — pregnancy, goals, experience level — but the builder is open-ended.

Do you take a cut of my sales?

No. LinkUp never processes your programme checkout. Visitors leave to your Stripe, Shopify, Kajabi, or whatever you already use. You keep 100%.

Can I still use a normal link list?

Yes. Switch to Hub mode in the studio anytime. Most sellers keep the quiz as the default.

How is this priced vs Linktree?

Linktree Pro is about $15/mo with seller fees if you sell through them. Premium is $35/mo for 0% fees. LinkUp Guide is €19/mo with a sales quiz they don’t have — and 0% on your off-platform sales at every tier.

Is building free?

Yes. Build and preview everything. You only pay when you publish on your address.

Do website builds charge online?

No. Sales pages and full sites are quoted by email to paidilyons6@gmail.com. In-app Stripe is only for LinkUp software (Launch / Guide / Scale) when someone publishes.

Questions

Answered plainly.

Do I need a website?

No. LinkUp is the website. One link in your Instagram or TikTok bio — change what’s behind it whenever you want.

What if I hate being on camera?

Skip the welcome video and the page opens straight onto your board. You can add one later — fifteen seconds is enough.

Can I change it after it’s live?

As often as you like. Edit on your phone and it’s updated for everyone immediately. The link never changes.

What happens to my email list if I leave?

You export it and take it with you. Addresses people gave you are yours, not ours.

Is there a free trial?

Building is free. You only pay when you want it live on your own address.

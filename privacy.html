Privacy

There are two kinds of people here, and they're treated differently: creators, who have an account and a page, and visitors, who open somebody's page.

If you're a visitor

Opening someone's LinkUp page sets no cookies and runs no tracking. We do not profile you, and nothing is shared with advertisers.

Two things are recorded:

  • Which bubble was tapped. Just the label and the time — no IP address, no device details, nothing that identifies you. It's a counter, so the creator can see what people are interested in.
  • Your email address, only if you type it in and submit the form. That goes to the creator whose page you were on, so they can email you. They control it, not us — we store it for them.

Every email sent through LinkUp carries an unsubscribe link that works immediately. You can also ask us directly at the address above.

If you're a creator

We hold your email address and password (hashed — we can't read it), whatever you put on your page, the files you upload, and your subscription status.

Payment card details never reach us. Stripe handles payment and stores the card; we keep only a customer reference and whether you're paid up.

The email addresses collected through your page are yours. You can download them at any time, and you're the data controller for that list — which means you're responsible for only mailing people who agreed, and for honouring unsubscribes. We record when and where each person agreed so you can show it.

Where it's kept

In the European Union. The database, files and authentication are hosted by Supabase in an EU region; the site is served by Netlify; payments by Stripe; email delivery by Resend. Stripe and Resend are US companies and transfer data under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

How long

Your account and page stay until you delete them. Deleting your account removes your page, your uploads, your email list and your history — immediately and permanently. Tap counts older than 30 days aren't shown and are periodically cleared. Records we're required to keep for accounting are kept for six years.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Deletion is a button in your account; the rest, email us and we'll answer within a month. If you're unhappy you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Cookies

None for advertising or analytics. Signing in stores a token in your browser so you stay signed in — that's necessary for the service to work and isn't used for anything else.
